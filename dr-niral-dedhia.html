Meet the doctor

Dr. Niral Dedhia

Founder & Lead Dentist | Dental Implant Specialist

  • B.D.S.Y.M.T. Dental College (MUHS, Nashik)

Dr. Niral Dedhia has been dedicated to transforming smiles and elevating the standard of personalised dental care in Mumbai.

Dental implants, smile rehabilitation and comprehensive oral care have been his focus for all his patients.

His decade of practice combines clinical experience, precision-driven treatment planning and a deep understanding of the individual needs of every patient.

His special interest in implant dentistry has led him to focus on providing carefully planned solutions for missing teeth, with an emphasis on aesthetics, function, precision and long-term oral health.

From diagnosis and treatment planning to restorative and implant procedures, the integration of modern digital technology enables greater precision, efficiency and predictability throughout the treatment journey.

The goal is not simply to provide dental treatment, but to create an experience where patients feel informed, comfortable, confident and cared for at every step.

He is a firm believer in “Dentistry That Goes Beyond Teeth.”
